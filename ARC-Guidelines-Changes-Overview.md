# ARC Guidelines Changes Overview
## Comparing September 2020 (Original) to July 2026 (Current)

This document provides an overview of all changes made to the Reserve at Minneola Architectural Guidelines from the original adoption (September 17, 2020) through the latest revision (July 2026).

---

## Document Structure Changes

### Header & Formatting
- **Title Format:** Changed from `## ARCHITECTURAL GUIDELINES` to `# Reserve at Minneola` with `# ARCHITECTURAL GUIDELINES`
- **Adopted Date:** Formatting updated from italic to bold
- **Revision Date:** Added **Revision Date:** July 2026
- **Table of Contents:** Added full TOC with anchor links (none existed before)
- **Definitions Section:** New comprehensive definitions section added

### Overall Organization
- **2020:** Sections in somewhat random order
- **2026:** Alphabetically organized sections, clearer hierarchy, consistent formatting

---

## Completely Removed Sections

### 1. Security/Video Cameras (Entire Section Removed)
The original 2020 guidelines contained an extensive section (7 subsections) governing security camera installation:

**Removed content included:**
- Requirement for ARC approval for camera installation
- Limit of 8 cameras per home (max 4 visible from front)
- Size restrictions (7" x 4" x 4" max)
- Concealment requirements for cables/conduit
- Mounting location restrictions (walls, soffit, fascia only - no rooftops)
- Privacy restrictions (no pointing at neighbor's doors/windows)
- Liability disclaimers

**Impact:** Homeowners now have more flexibility with security camera installation without ARC oversight.

### 2. Signage (Partial Section Removed)
The 2020 version included under Sidewalks section:
> "4. Other signs are not permitted without ARC approval."

This provision was removed in the current version.

### 3. Portable Storage/Moving Containers (Standalone Section Removed)
The 2020 version had a separate section stating containers were permitted for a maximum of seven (7) days. This was consolidated into the Temporary Structures section.

---

## New Sections Added

### 1. Definitions Section (Entirely New)
Added clear definitions for key terms:

- **Common Areas:** All property within Reserve at Minneola owned/maintained by Association
- **Distinctive Flora:** Native Florida plants protected by state/local ordinances
- **Like New Condition:** Detailed maintenance standard for fences, play structures, painted surfaces, and screening
- **Substantially Similar:** Materials matching original in appearance, quality, function

### 2. Screened Entry of Home (Renamed & Reorganized)
- Previously titled "Front Entry of Home"
- Moved to be near other screen-related sections

### 3. Hurricane Protection (New Section)
Replaced the limited "Storm/Hurricane Shutters" section. Current version covers:
- Storm shutters, impact-resistant windows and doors, and reinforced garage doors
- Color matching requirements for permanent shutters
- Timeline for temporary shutter installation/removal (72 hours before, 14 days after)

### 4. Temporary Structures (New Section)
Consolidates temporary structure rules:
- **Portable Storage/Moving Containers:** Extended from 7 days to 1 month, must be on driveway
- **Construction Dumpsters:** 1 month maximum, must be on driveway
- **Temporary Construction Shelters:** 1 month maximum during active construction
- General safety requirements (no blocking sidewalks, secure against wind, homeowner liability)

### 5. Solar Panels and Solar Collectors (Dedicated Section)
Separated from the previous combined "Gutters and Solar Collectors" section. Current version:
- References Florida Statute 163.04
- Roof placement guidance (rear and side roofs when possible)
- Equipment color matching requirements
- Building code and maintenance requirements

## Significantly Modified Sections

### 1. Introduction Section
Added language regarding Florida statutes:
> "To the extent that Florida statutes limit the Association's authority to regulate certain items or installations, such statutes shall prevail."

**Impact:** Acknowledges that state law supersedes HOA restrictions where applicable.

### 2. Alteration Application Process
- Renamed from "Alteration Application" to "Alteration Application Process"
- Added clarification that applications are deemed rejected (not approved) after 30 days
- Added comprehensive documentation requirements table mapping each alteration type to required documents
- Added simplified "Like-for-Like Replacements" path for identical replacements

### 3. Exterior Painting and Approved Color Schemes
- Removed specific color prohibition language (Salmon, Dutch Tile Blue)
- Added option for homeowners to submit photos of color swatches when repainting same colors
- Simplified approval process for repainting same colors

### 4. Fencing Section (Reorganized)
- Added clear "Fence Setback Requirements" subsection with consolidated rules
- Added "Primary Rule" and "Exception" formatting
- Reorganized corner lot information into dedicated subsection
- Added explicit "Front Yard Restriction" statement
- Moved "Like New Condition" definition to Definitions section
- All actual fence rules and specifications carried over unchanged

### 5. Flags Section
- Title simplified from "Flags - Federal, State, Military" to "Flags"
- Content simplified to single reference to Florida Statute 720.304

### 6. Generators
- Added natural gas as a fuel option (was propane only)
- Added fencing as a screening option (was shrubs/landscaping only)

### 7. Gutters (Split from Solar)
- 2020: Combined "Gutters and Solar Collectors" section
- 2026: Separate "Gutters" section; solar moved to dedicated section

### 8. Lawns
- Removed requirement for rear yards to be grassed
- Rear yards now subject to drainage requirements only

### 9. Outbuildings, Sheds and Storage Containers
- 2020: "No temporary outbuildings, sheds, or other outdoor storage containers shall be permitted"
- 2026: "Sheds and storage containers visible from streets, adjacent parcels, or common areas require ARC approval"
- Major policy shift: sheds/storage containers now permitted, just need approval if visible

### 10. Roofs (Consolidated)
- "Front Roof Changes" is now a subsection under "Roofs" (was separate section)
- Added: "All shingle replacements must be dimensional with a thirty (30) year life"

### 11. Windows - Replacement, Tinting and Treatments
- Added provision for impact-resistant windows and doors meeting Florida Building Code

## Summary of Key Policy Changes

### More Permissive Changes (Homeowner-Friendly)
1. Security cameras - No longer require ARC approval (section removed)
2. Sheds/storage containers - Now permitted if not visible or with approval (was prohibited)
3. Portable storage containers (PODS) - Extended from 7 days to 1 month
4. Paint colors - Easier to maintain existing scheme without full re-approval
5. Rear yard grass - No longer required to be grassed (flexibility added)
6. Solar panels - Explicit Florida Statute 163.04 compliance
7. Generators - Can now use natural gas; fencing added as screening option

### Clarifications & Documentation Improvements
1. Definitions section - Clear definitions of key terms
2. Fence setback rules - Consolidated and clarified
3. Hurricane protection - Clear coverage of storm shutters and protection measures
4. Temporary structures - Clear time limits and requirements
5. Table of contents - Easier navigation
6. Documentation requirements table - Clear checklist for applicants
7. Like-for-Like replacement path - Simplified process for identical replacements

### Florida Statute Compliance Updates
- FS 720.304 - Flag display rights
- FS 720.3075 - Florida-friendly landscaping rights
- FS 163.04 - Solar energy device rights
- Florida Building Code - Various construction requirements

## Sections Trimmed in July 2026 Revision

Based on HOA Board feedback that the January 2026 revision introduced too many changes at once, the following sections were simplified:

1. **Flags:** Trimmed from 3 points to 1 (single statute reference; maintenance covered by general Exterior Maintenance section)
2. **Florida-Friendly Landscaping:** Removed standalone subsection with application bullet points; replaced with single line referencing FS 720.3075 (existing FYN application process in Landscaping section already covers this)
3. **Solar Panels and Solar Collectors:** Trimmed from 4 subsections to 2 points (removed ground-mounted section, roof warranty disclaimer, "integrated architecture" language, and 30-day repair/removal requirement)
4. **Hurricane Protection:** Trimmed from comprehensive multi-subsection format (covered measures list, permanent/temporary shutter specs, impact windows, garage doors, erosion controls, general requirements) to 2 points (core policy + timeline only; generator/fuel tank coverage lives in Generators section, maintenance in Exterior Maintenance section)
5. **Gas Tanks:** Removed HB 293 fuel tank exception (already covered by Generators and Hurricane Protection sections)
6. **Temporary Structures:** Trimmed from 4 subsections with bullet lists to 3 points (same coverage, less formatting overhead)
